# Product Rally

> A community-driven board for discovering, posting, and voting on design and maker tools.

## 🔗 Links

* **Website:** https://productrally.com/

## 📌 What is Product Rally?

Product Rally is a **weekly discovery board for tools**.

Users can post and vote on products and resources they find useful.

According to its current project description, the platform covers hundreds of tools across multiple categories. ([deny.works](https://deny.works/))

## 🔄 How It Works

```text
Discover Tool
     ↓
Submit Product
     ↓
Community Votes
     ↓
Weekly Ranking
     ↓
Discover New Tools
```

The platform runs a new board each week, allowing recently submitted tools to gain visibility. ([craftworkdesign](https://www6.twstalker.com/craftworkdesign))
